An allocation is useful when you can explain where it came from, compare it fairly and see where it may fail.

Rocky Finance is a portfolio research application. It connects household survey modelling, constrained allocation and scenario analysis. Its outputs support investigation; they do not establish that a strategy will outperform or that it suits your complete financial situation.

01 · DATA

Every calculation belongs to a data version.

Research uses complete monthly returns from a versioned daily stock-price snapshot, not live quotes. The active universe originated from a fixed list of 503 US stock symbols. It is not a point-in-time reconstruction of historical index membership. Retired or stale securities are excluded from current allocations, so selection and survivorship bias remain.

Data is downloaded from public Yahoo Finance chart history, with a separately identified public archive fallback where necessary. The importer records provider, price-adjustment basis, symbol identity, coverage and a file fingerprint. It rejects incomplete or inconsistent refreshes and does not fill missing closing prices. Public availability is not a redistribution licence; raw market files are not published in the code repository.

Each result shows the actual calibration dates and sample size. The monthly panel excludes the incomplete current month. A current quote does not update an old calculation. Adjusted prices are used according to the recorded provider basis; corporate actions and provider revisions can still affect the history. A fingerprint identifies data; it does not independently certify its accuracy.

02 · ALLOCATION

A defined optimization problem, not a stock-picking promise.

The engine searches within your chosen stocks. Holdings must be non-negative, sum to 100% and respect your holding cap. Minimum variance uses estimated covariance only. The target-return method adds a return constraint, interpolated between the minimum-variance mean and the highest feasible mean at the same cap.

Minimize wᵀΣw
subject to Σwᵢ = 1, 0 ≤ wᵢ ≤ holding cap
and, when selected, μᵀw ≥ return target.

Why shrink estimates? Short histories make means and covariances noisy. We use Ledoit–Wolf covariance shrinkage toward a scaled identity matrix. Asset means shrink toward the cross-asset average according to an uncertainty heuristic. This reduces reliance on some noisy estimates; it does not turn historical returns into reliable forecasts.

Annual arithmetic means are 12 times monthly means. Annual covariance is 12 times monthly covariance; volatility is √(wᵀΣw). Effective holdings = 1 / Σwᵢ². A stock's share of variance = wᵢ(Σw)ᵢ / (wᵀΣw). More ticker names do not necessarily mean more independent risks.

The household ExtraTrees model estimates observed risky-asset ownership in the 2022 US Survey of Consumer Finances. It is a survey proxy, not an individual suitability assessment. It does not measure emergency reserves, investment horizon or capacity for loss. Its initial research preference can be changed; the output is not a recommended stock share.

03 · EVIDENCE

Compare the same dates, rules and costs.

The rolling evaluation uses the last 24 common monthly returns. Each month's weights use only preceding observations. Selected method, equal weights and minimum variance share the same stocks, dates and trading-cost assumptions. This separation prevents future returns from entering the fitting window, but today's stock selection and preferences still introduce bias.

Gross turnover = sum of absolute changes from drifted holdings to target weights.
Net monthly return = (1 − turnover × fee bps / 10,000) × (1 + portfolio return) − 1.

Costs include initial entry and gross buys plus sells; there is no terminal liquidation, tax or market-impact model. CAGR compounds net returns. Drawdown uses monthly closes and can miss larger intramonth declines. Lower risk can come with lower returns. Twenty-four months are insufficient evidence of a persistent advantage.

Predeclared checks compare 0, 10, 25 and 50 bps plus your selected cost; 36, 60, 84 and 120 months plus your selected window; and 25%, 35% plus your selected holding cap. Unavailable or infeasible cases are labelled. None is chosen automatically because its result looks best.

Weight stability uses 128 joint circular block resamples, three-month blocks and a fixed seed. Each resample refits means, covariance and weights under the same policy. The middle 80% range describes sensitivity to this sample, not a confidence interval for future optimal weights.

04 · SIMULATION

A goal fraction is conditional on the model.

Quick exploration resamples all assets together in three-month blocks. This preserves contemporaneous dependence and some short patterns. It uses empirical returns without mean shrinkage. Repeating an exceptional five-year period for decades can produce very high outcomes; more simulated paths do not validate that assumption.

Real wealth at year t = nominal wealth / (1 + inflation rate)^t.
Goal fraction = number of paths ending at or above the real target / total paths.

A target of $10,000 in today's money means preserving that amount of purchasing power. It does not mean reaching $100,000. Results display the exact target and horizon alongside tail wealth, maximum investment drawdown and the fraction of paths that fall at least 30% from an earlier peak. A high terminal-goal fraction can coexist with large interim losses.

Baseline and ±1 percentage point mean cases are sensitivity comparisons, without assigned likelihood. Statistical and manual cases also scale volatility by 0.9 / 1.1. Wider historical-model checks subtract 10 or 20 annualized arithmetic percentage points from monthly means. These are deliberate stress assumptions, not predicted bear markets.

A separate mean-uncertainty experiment estimates one joint block-bootstrap mean per path and holds its difference from the original sample mean fixed throughout that path. It uses independent resampling and the same baseline market innovations. This is a simple conditional sensitivity experiment, not a calibrated predictive probability or an institutional forecasting model.

Fixed historical replays apply today's weights with monthly rebalancing during specified crisis windows. They exclude cash flows and costs. If any selected stock lacks the complete event history, the event is unavailable; no proxy is inserted. These are hindsight stress illustrations, not investable backtests.

Simulation fees and taxes are excluded. Cash flows occur at month end; fixed payments can increase after completed years. Normal models can generate returns below −100%, floored at total loss; lognormal models keep prices positive. GARCH captures fitted volatility clustering but its parameters can be uncertain in short samples. Wilson intervals describe finite simulation sampling error only.

05 · TRACEABILITY

Keep the evidence behind a result.

Download a calculation record from the results. It contains normalized inputs, data identifiers, engine version, fitted parameters and diagnostic outputs. Allocation receipts include annual means and the covariance matrix in ticker order. Simulation receipts freeze the actual model, random seed, weights, horizon, cash flows, inflation and goal. Editing a form does not rewrite the record.

Reproduction requires the same data and numerical dependencies. The identifier fingerprints the recorded inputs and calibration; it is not an external audit, security certificate or investment guarantee. Household profile fields and login credentials are excluded from this export.

Inspect the source · Inspect automated verification. Tests cover mathematical invariants, identical market paths, cash flows, costs, data handling and the application workflow. Passing tests does not establish profitable trading or future predictive accuracy.

Research that informs the design

  • DeMiguel, Garlappi & Uppal (2009): estimated optimizers did not consistently beat equal weighting across their tested samples. This motivates retaining a simple baseline, not claiming equal weight is always best.
  • Ledoit & Wolf, covariance shrinkage: noisy covariance estimates can be amplified by optimization. Our implementation uses the scikit-learn scaled-identity estimator; covariance shrinkage does not solve long-term mean estimation.
  • BlackRock capital market assumptions: mean-estimation uncertainty is distinct from return volatility. Our diagnostics borrow that distinction, not their proprietary forecasting model.
  • Vanguard model notes: conditional simulations can underestimate negative extremes absent from historical data. Model fractions need this context.
  • Federal Reserve 2022 Survey of Consumer Finances: source for the household ownership proxy.

These authors and institutions have not reviewed or endorsed Rocky Finance.
